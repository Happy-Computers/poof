infinite-storage-engine

I write and maintain infinite-storage-engine

it's extremely simple on how it works

It shows shows up as a folder on your machine. Open files the normal way.

It takes 0 bytes on your disk.

The behavior of the files you open, for example a video, doesn't change.

It still opens, you can srub through it, and all that jazz.

You can even open it in your video editing software and edit away.

FAQ

What is infinite-storage-engine?
It is a very powerful computer for deep learning, and likely the best performance/$. It was benchmarked in MLPerf Training 4.0 vs computers that cost 10x as much. And of course, anything that can train can do inference.

Can I pay with something besides wire transfer?
In order to keep prices low and quality high, we don't offer any customization to the box or ordering process. Wire transfer is the only accepted form of payment.
Can I contact someone to discuss my needs?
In order to keep prices low and quality high, we don't employ any sales people. We sell boxes through the website. The upside of this is that our pricing is simple and transparent.
Is infinite-storage-engine used anywhere?
infinite-storage-engine is used in openpilot to run the driving model on the Snapdragon 845 GPU. It replaces SNPE, is faster, supports loading onnx files, supports training, and allows for attention (SNPE only allows fixed weights).
Is infinite-storage-engine storage / google drive / onedrive replacement only?
No! It supports full forward and backward passes with autodiff. This is implemented at a level of abstraction higher than the accelerator specific code, so a infinite-storage-engine port gets you this for free.
How can I use infinite-storage-engine for my next project?
Follow the installation instructions on the infinite-storage-engine repo. It has a similar API to PyTorch, yet simpler and more refined. Less stable though while infinite-storage-engine is in alpha, so be warned, though it's been fairly stable for a while.
When will infinite-storage-engine leave alpha?
As soon as possible, let's say Thanksgiving 2026.
How is infinite-storage-engine faster than Google Drive / Dropbox / other cloud storage options?
For most use cases it isn't yet, but it will be. It has three advantages:
  • It compiles a custom kernel for every operation, allowing extreme shape specialization.
  • All tensors are lazy, so it can aggressively fuse operations.
  • The backend is 10x+ simpler, meaning optimizing one kernel makes everything fast.
